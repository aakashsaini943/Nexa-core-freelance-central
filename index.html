❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start building something extraordinary together.

How quickly can NexaCore start on my project?+
For most engagements, we can onboard a dedicated team within 7–10 business days. Enterprise contracts with custom SLAs may require an initial discovery sprint of 2 weeks before full team deployment.
Do you work with existing technology stacks?+
Absolutely. Our architects perform a comprehensive tech audit in week one. We integrate with and modernize legacy systems rather than forcing wholesale rewrites — protecting your existing investments while preparing for future scale.
What engagement models do you offer?+
We offer three primary models: Fixed-price project delivery, Time & Materials for evolving scopes, and Dedicated Team augmentation. We recommend the best model after understanding your goals, budget, and timeline.
How do you ensure data security and confidentiality?+
We are ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 Type II compliant. Every engagement begins with an NDA and IP assignment agreement. We use VPNs, MFA, zero-trust access, and encrypted repositories for all client codebases.
Can you scale the team up or down during the project?+
Yes. One of the key advantages of partnering with NexaCore is elastic team scaling. We can add senior engineers, QA specialists, DevOps leads, or data scientists within 5 business days based on your evolving needs.
What post-delivery support do you provide?+
All projects include a 90-day hypercare period post-launch with priority bug fixes included. Beyond that, we offer tiered managed services contracts covering monitoring, security patching, feature development, and infrastructure management.
